About Us — Knoxville Pressure Washing Specialists

Knox Pressure Pros is a locally operated exterior cleaning service built around one principle: the price we quote is the price you pay.

Most Knoxville homeowners dealing with algae, mildew, and East Tennessee's perennial spring pollen problem have had the same experience: a contractor who shows up when they feel like it, quotes one number and invoices another, or does work so aggressive it leaves marks on the siding. We built this business around a simpler idea — transparent pricing, proper technique for every surface type, and work you can actually see from the street. We're not the cheapest option in the Knoxville market. We are the option that shows up when we say we will, uses the right cleaning method for each surface, and stands behind the result.

What We Do

We specialize in residential and light commercial pressure washing for Knoxville and surrounding East Tennessee communities — Farragut, Maryville, Oak Ridge, Powell, Halls, Bearden, Fountain City, and beyond. Services include house soft washing, driveway and concrete cleaning, deck and patio cleaning, roof soft washing, fence cleaning, and full-property packages. Every service uses biodegradable cleaning solutions and calibrated technique by surface type.

What Makes Us Different

Written fixed-price quotes: Before we start any job, you receive a written quote. That number doesn't change when we finish. If we encounter something unexpected mid-job — a section of siding that needs different handling, heavier moss on one roof section than visible from the ground — we stop, show you what we found, and get your sign-off before proceeding. No invoice surprises.

Correct technique by surface type: Soft wash for vinyl siding, wood, stucco, and roofs. Calibrated pressure (2,500–3,500 PSI with a rotating surface cleaner) for concrete and pavers. Wrong technique causes real damage — stripped granules on shingles, cracked siding seams, displaced paver joint sand. We use the right method every time, not the fastest one.

We answer the phone: Call (865) 378-8377 and someone picks up or calls back within the hour. We don't make you wait three days for a quote or leave you wondering if the job is still scheduled. Communication is part of the service.

Who We Serve

We serve homeowners and businesses across Knoxville, Farragut, Maryville, Oak Ridge, Powell, Halls, Bearden, Fountain City, Karns, West Knoxville, Lenoir City, Sevierville, and the surrounding East Tennessee area. Most jobs are scheduled within 3–5 business days. If you're outside our primary service area and need pressure washing, call us — we may still be able to help depending on job scope.

Our Commitment

Every job gets a written quote before any work starts. If we find something unexpected, we stop and show you before any additional work proceeds. The price we quote is the price you pay. If something doesn't meet your expectations when we're done, we come back and make it right — that's standard, not a special exception.

Ready to Get Started?

The estimate is free, no obligation, and comes with a written quote you can keep. Call or request online — we respond within the hour.
