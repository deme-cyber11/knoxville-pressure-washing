Soft Wash Roof Cleaning in Knoxville, TN — Protect Your Shingles

Safely removes black streaks, Gloeocapsa Magma algae, moss, and lichen using the ARMA-recommended soft wash method. No high-pressure blasting that damages shingles or voids warranties.

When You Need Roof Cleaning

Dark streaks running down your shingles — especially on north-facing or shaded sections — are the first sign. That's Gloeocapsa Magma, a blue-green algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles and thrives in Knoxville's humid climate. It spreads from the shaded side of the roof outward across the entire surface over one to three seasons. Beyond the visible staining, the algae actively degrades your roof: it accelerates granule loss from shingles (those granules are the UV and waterproofing layer), and as the growth spreads, it creates conditions where moss and lichen can establish. Lichen physically etches shingle material with root-like holdfasts. Homeowners who wait lose years of roof life — and sometimes end up replacing a roof that could have been cleaned.

Why Method Matters — Soft Wash vs. Pressure

The most common mistake in roof cleaning is using the wrong tool. High-pressure washing (1,500+ PSI) on asphalt shingles blasts granules off the surface, forces water under shingle overlaps, and voids most manufacturer warranties. Soft washing uses 60–200 PSI — barely more pressure than a garden hose — combined with professional cleaning solutions that kill algae, moss, and bacteria at the cellular level. The solution does the cleaning; the water is just the delivery and rinse mechanism. This is the method recommended by the Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association (ARMA). We follow it on every roof job.

How Roof Cleaning Works

1Prepare: We protect landscaping at the drip line, close windows, and cover any sensitive surfaces below the roofline before applying any solution.

2Apply cleaning solution: A professional sodium hypochlorite-based solution is applied to the entire roof surface and allowed to dwell long enough to kill all organic growth — algae, moss, and lichen — completely.

3Moss removal: Any large moss clumps are carefully removed by hand before the final rinse to avoid debris buildup in gutters.

4Low-pressure rinse: The roof is gently rinsed at soft wash pressure. Black streaks and algae disappear immediately. Any remaining residue fades over the following weeks as dead growth washes away with rain.

5Inspect and confirm: We walk the job with you before leaving.

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes black streaks on Knoxville roofs?

Black streaks on asphalt shingles are Gloeocapsa Magma — a blue-green algae that feeds on limestone filler in shingles. Knoxville's humidity and warm temperatures make it ideal for this algae to establish and spread. It typically starts on north-facing or shaded sections and expands across the whole roof if untreated. Left unchecked, it accelerates granule loss and shortens roof life by years.

Is soft washing safe for asphalt shingles?

Yes. Soft washing is the ARMA-recommended cleaning method for asphalt shingles. It uses low pressure (60–200 PSI) and professional cleaning solutions to kill algae and moss without dislodging granules or voiding warranties. We never use pressure washers directly on roof shingle surfaces — that causes the exact damage homeowners are trying to avoid.

How long does roof cleaning take?

Most Knoxville roofs are cleaned in 2–4 hours depending on size and pitch. The results are visible immediately on most roofs. Any remaining residue continues to fade over the next few weeks as dead organic growth washes off with rain.

Do I need to be home during roof cleaning?

No. We need access to a water spigot. We'll let you know before we start and leave notes if we observe anything worth flagging during the job.
