What Is House Washing?

House washing is a specialized exterior cleaning service designed to safely and effectively remove dirt, grime, mold, mildew, algae, pollen, spiderwebs, and other organic growth from your home's siding. Unlike high-pressure power washing that can damage delicate surfaces, Knox Pressure Pros primarily utilizes a soft washing technique. This method involves applying a biodegradable, eco-friendly cleaning solution at low pressure, similar to a garden hose. The solution breaks down contaminants at a molecular level, allowing them to be gently rinsed away without abrasive force. We use professional-grade detergents tailored for various siding materials, including vinyl, stucco, brick, Hardie board, and wood, ensuring a thorough clean that protects your home's surfaces while delivering stunning results.

Common Questions About House Washing

How much does House Washing cost in Knoxville, TN?

The cost of house washing in Knoxville, TN, can vary based on several factors, including the size and height of your home, the type of siding material, and the extent of dirt and organic growth. On average, homeowners in the Knoxville area can expect to pay anywhere from $250 to $600 for a professional house wash. We provide free, no-obligation estimates tailored to your specific property to give you an accurate quote.

How long does House Washing take?

The duration of a professional house washing service typically depends on the size of your home and the level of accumulated grime. Most standard-sized homes in Knoxville can be thoroughly cleaned within 2 to 4 hours. Larger properties or those with significant buildup of mold and mildew may require additional time, but we always work efficiently to minimize disruption to your day.

How do I know if I need House Washing?

There are several clear signs that your Knoxville home could benefit from a professional house wash. Look for green or black streaks, particularly on the shady sides of your house, indicating algae and mildew growth. Faded or dull-looking siding, visible dirt and cobwebs, or reddish-brown staining from soil splash-back are also strong indicators. If your home simply doesn't look as vibrant as it once did, it's likely time for a professional cleaning.
