How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Knoxville? (2026 Guide)

Published February 23, 2026 · by Knox Pressure Pros

If you're a homeowner in Knoxville, TN and your home's exterior is looking a little worse for wear, you've probably wondered: how much does pressure washing cost in Knoxville? Whether it's green algae creeping up your siding, oil stains on your driveway, or black streaks on your roof, professional pressure washing can restore your property's curb appeal — but what should you expect to pay?

In this 2026 pricing guide, we'll break down the average cost of pressure washing services in the Knoxville area so you can budget with confidence before requesting your free estimate.

Average Pressure Washing Prices in Knoxville (2026)

Pressure washing costs vary depending on the surface being cleaned, the size of the area, the level of buildup, and whether standard high-pressure or soft washing is needed. Here are the typical price ranges you'll see from reputable Knoxville pressure washing companies in 2026:

  • House Washing: $250 – $600 for an average single-story home. Two-story homes typically run $400 – $800. Most house washing in Knoxville uses a soft wash method to protect siding materials.
  • Driveway Cleaning: $150 – $350 depending on the size and condition. A standard two-car driveway typically costs around $150 – $200.
  • Deck Cleaning: $175 – $400 based on square footage and material. Composite decks may cost slightly more due to the care required.
  • Roof Cleaning: $350 – $700. Roof cleaning always uses low-pressure soft washing to avoid damaging shingles.
  • Fence Cleaning: $125 – $350 depending on the linear footage and material.
  • Commercial Pressure Washing: Prices vary widely based on the scope. Storefronts might start at $200, while full parking lot cleaning can run $500+.

What Factors Affect the Cost?

No two pressure washing jobs are identical. Here are the biggest factors that influence pricing in the Knoxville market:

1. Square Footage

This is the most straightforward factor. Larger homes, longer driveways, and bigger decks simply take more time, more water, and more cleaning solution. Most Knoxville pressure washing companies price by square footage or provide a flat rate based on a visual estimate.

2. Type of Surface

Concrete driveways can handle high-pressure cleaning, but vinyl siding, painted wood, and roof shingles require soft washing — a gentler technique that uses specialized cleaning solutions at low pressure. Soft washing typically costs a bit more because of the chemicals and expertise involved.

3. Level of Buildup and Staining

A home that hasn't been cleaned in five years will cost more than one on a regular maintenance schedule. Heavy mold, mildew, algae, and oil stains require extra treatment passes and stronger cleaning agents. Knoxville's humid subtropical climate means organic growth is extremely common, especially on north-facing surfaces.

4. Accessibility

Multi-story homes, steep roofs, and properties with difficult access (tight lots, extensive landscaping) may require additional equipment or time, which can affect pricing.

5. Bundling Services

Many Knoxville homeowners save money by bundling multiple services. For example, getting your house washed, driveway cleaned, and deck restored in one visit often qualifies for a package discount. At Knox Pressure Pros, we always look for ways to give you the best value.

Pressure Washing Cost Per Square Foot

Some companies quote by the square foot. In the Knoxville area, typical rates are:

  • Flatwork (driveways, sidewalks, patios): $0.15 – $0.30 per sq ft
  • House washing (soft wash): $0.20 – $0.40 per sq ft
  • Deck cleaning: $0.25 – $0.50 per sq ft
  • Roof cleaning: $0.30 – $0.50 per sq ft

Is Professional Pressure Washing Worth the Cost?

Absolutely. Beyond the obvious aesthetic improvement, professional pressure washing protects your investment. Mold, mildew, and algae cause real damage over time — they degrade siding, eat into wood, and shorten roof life. Regular exterior cleaning extends the life of your home's surfaces and can even increase your property value by 5-10% according to real estate studies.

Plus, hiring a professional eliminates the risk of damage from incorrect pressure settings, which can gouge wood, strip paint, or crack concrete. A professional crew with commercial-grade equipment can finish in a fraction of the time it would take to DIY.

How to Get the Best Price on Pressure Washing in Knoxville

Here are our top tips for getting great value:

  • Get multiple estimates: Compare at least 2-3 quotes from licensed and insured companies.
  • Bundle services: Combine house washing with driveway or deck cleaning for a discount.
  • Schedule in the off-season: Late fall and winter are slower periods — you may get better pricing. Check out our guide on the best time to pressure wash in Knoxville.
  • Maintain regularly: Annual or biannual cleaning prevents heavy buildup, which keeps costs lower. Learn how often to pressure wash your driveway.
  • Avoid the cheapest bid: Extremely low quotes often mean uninsured operators or contractors who cut corners.
