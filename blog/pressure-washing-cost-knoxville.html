How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Knoxville? (2026)

If you're a homeowner in Knoxville, TN, you've probably noticed how quickly East Tennessee's humidity can leave your home's exterior looking dingy. Algae on the siding, black streaks on the roof, oil stains on the driveway — it adds up. But before you pick up the phone, you probably want to know: how much is this going to cost?

Here's a straightforward breakdown of pressure washing prices in the Knoxville area for 2026, based on the most common residential jobs we handle.

Average Pressure Washing Prices in Knoxville

Pricing depends on the surface being cleaned, the square footage, and the level of buildup. Here are typical ranges you can expect from reputable Knoxville pressure washing companies:

  • House Washing (Soft Wash): $250–$500 for an average-sized home (1,500–2,500 sq ft). Larger homes or multi-story properties can run $500–$800.
  • Driveway Cleaning: $150–$300 depending on size and staining. A standard two-car driveway typically falls around $150–$200.
  • Deck Cleaning: $175–$350 based on deck size and material. Wood decks that need special treatment may cost more than composite.
  • Roof Cleaning: $350–$600 for most residential roofs. This is always done with soft wash to protect shingles.
  • Fence Cleaning: $125–$300 depending on linear footage and material type.
  • Commercial Properties: Custom quotes based on scope, but storefronts typically start around $200–$400.

What Affects the Price?

Several factors can push your quote higher or lower:

Square footage is the biggest factor. More surface area means more time, water, and cleaning solution. A 3,000 sq ft house will naturally cost more than a 1,200 sq ft ranch.

Level of buildup matters too. A driveway that hasn't been cleaned in five years with deep oil stains requires more passes and possibly pre-treatment compared to annual maintenance cleaning.

Accessibility plays a role. Two-story homes require extension equipment. Properties with landscaping close to the house may need extra care to protect plants. Steep driveways or roofs take longer to clean safely.

Surface type determines the method. Soft washing (low pressure with cleaning solutions) is required for roofs, vinyl siding, and painted surfaces. It takes more time than straight high-pressure cleaning on concrete.

Bundled services often save money. If you're getting your house, driveway, and deck done at the same time, most companies offer package pricing that's cheaper than booking each separately.

How Knoxville Compares to National Averages

The good news for Knoxville homeowners is that pressure washing prices here tend to be slightly below national averages. According to industry data, the national average for house washing is $300–$600, while Knoxville typically comes in at the lower end of that range. Our cost of living and competitive local market help keep prices reasonable.

Is It Worth the Investment?

Absolutely. Beyond the obvious curb appeal boost, regular pressure washing protects your investment. Algae and mildew can degrade siding and roofing materials over time. Mold on decks creates slipping hazards. Dirty driveways can actually reduce your home's resale value.

Real estate agents in Knox County consistently report that homes with clean exteriors sell faster and often for more. A $300 house wash before listing is one of the highest-ROI improvements you can make.
