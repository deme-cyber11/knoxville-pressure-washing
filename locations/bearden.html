Bearden Pressure Washing Experts

Bearden is one of Knoxville's most walkable and vibrant neighborhoods — home to the shops and restaurants along Kingston Pike, the historic Bearden Village district, and beautiful residential streets with homes ranging from 1940s bungalows to contemporary builds. The mature landscaping and canopy trees that make Bearden so charming also contribute to algae, mildew, and organic staining on exterior surfaces.

We help Bearden homeowners and businesses maintain clean, inviting properties. From the brick storefronts along Kingston Pike to the residential streets off Sutherland Avenue, we handle properties of all sizes.

Popular Bearden Services

  • House washing — Safe for historic brick, painted wood, and vinyl
  • Driveway & walkway cleaning — Remove stains from older concrete
  • Deck & patio cleaning — Restore outdoor living spaces
  • Commercial cleaning — Storefronts, sidewalks, parking areas
  • Roof cleaning — Black streak and moss removal

Why Bearden Properties Need Extra Care

Bearden's tree-lined streets mean more shade and more moisture on surfaces. The neighborhood's older homes often have textured brick and stone that traps dirt and organic growth more than modern smooth siding. We adjust our pressure and technique for each surface type — gentle soft wash for historic brick and painted wood, higher pressure for concrete and stone.

For Bearden businesses, we offer early-morning and weekend scheduling so cleaning doesn't disrupt customer traffic.
