Maryville Pressure Washing Experts

Maryville — the seat of Blount County and gateway to the Great Smoky Mountains — sits in one of Tennessee's most scenic locations. But that proximity to the Smokies also means more rainfall, higher humidity, and more aggressive organic growth on exterior surfaces than anywhere else in the Knoxville metro area.

Homes in Maryville's historic downtown, the neighborhoods around Maryville College, and the newer developments along Alcoa Highway and Lamar Alexander Parkway all deal with the same issue: green algae, black streaks, and mildew that make properties look neglected far faster than in drier climates.

Services in Maryville

  • Driveway & sidewalk cleaning — Remove algae and stains
  • Deck & patio cleaning — Essential for Maryville's outdoor-living lifestyle
  • Fence cleaning — Wood, vinyl, and aluminum
  • Roof cleaning — Black streak removal from shingles
  • Commercial — Downtown storefronts, Alcoa Highway businesses, hospitality properties

Smoky Mountain Humidity = More Cleaning

Maryville averages nearly 50 inches of rainfall per year — significantly more than Knoxville proper. That extra moisture, combined with shade from the foothills, means surfaces stay damp longer and organic growth happens faster. Most Maryville homeowners benefit from at least annual exterior cleaning, and properties with heavy tree cover may need it twice a year.

Maryville & Blount County Coverage

We serve all of Maryville and Blount County including downtown Maryville, Alcoa, Friendsville, Louisville, and the Foothills Parkway corridor. Whether you're near the college or out toward Townsend, we've got you covered.
