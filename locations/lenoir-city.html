Lenoir City's Trusted Pressure Washing Experts

Lenoir City is a charming Loudon County city of approximately 10,000 residents situated on the shores of Fort Loudoun Lake. With a rich railroad history dating back to the Lenoir City Rail Station, this community has evolved into a desirable lakefront destination and serves as the gateway to Tellico Village, one of Tennessee's premier retirement communities. Waterfront living along Fort Loudoun Lake is a major draw, but the constant lake moisture, combined with East Tennessee's humidity and pollen seasons, means driveways, docks, siding, and decks accumulate algae, mildew, and organic staining faster than properties further from the water.

Knox Pressure Pros delivers licensed, insured, and eco-friendly pressure washing throughout Lenoir City and Loudon County. Whether you own a lakefront home on Fort Loudoun Lake, a property in Tellico Village, or a business along Highway 321, we provide thorough cleaning that restores and protects your investment.

Our Pressure Washing Process

  1. Free Property Assessment — We evaluate your surfaces, identify moisture damage and staining patterns, and provide a clear, no-obligation quote.
  2. Equipment Setup & Area Prep — Landscaping, dock areas, and outdoor fixtures are protected before we stage our professional-grade equipment.
  3. Pre-Treatment Application — Lake-safe, biodegradable detergents are applied to dissolve algae, mold, mildew, and embedded organic stains.
  4. High-Pressure Cleaning — Calibrated pressure matched to each surface type removes buildup from concrete, wood, vinyl, brick, and composite materials.
  5. Final Rinse & Inspection — A thorough rinse and walk-through inspection ensure every surface meets our quality standards.
