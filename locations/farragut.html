Farragut's Trusted Pressure Washing Experts

Farragut is one of the most affluent suburbs in the Knoxville metro, spanning both Knox and Loudon counties with a population of roughly 23,000 residents. Named after Civil War Admiral David Farragut, this community is known for its top-rated schools, the bustling Turkey Creek shopping area, and established neighborhoods lined with mature trees. All that lush canopy creates shade and moisture that accelerates algae, mildew, and organic staining on driveways, siding, and decks throughout the year.

Knox Pressure Pros provides licensed, insured, and eco-friendly pressure washing throughout Farragut. Whether you need to restore your home's curb appeal before a sale, keep up with HOA standards, or clean up commercial property near Turkey Creek, we deliver results that last.

Our Pressure Washing Process

  1. Free Property Assessment — We inspect your surfaces, identify problem areas, and provide an upfront quote with no hidden fees.
  2. Equipment Setup & Area Prep — We protect landscaping, cover fixtures, and stage professional-grade equipment for safe, efficient cleaning.
  3. Pre-Treatment Application — Eco-friendly detergents are applied to break down algae, mold, mildew, and stubborn stains before washing begins.
  4. High-Pressure Cleaning — Using calibrated pressure levels matched to each surface type, we remove buildup without damaging your property.
  5. Final Rinse & Inspection — A thorough rinse followed by a walk-through inspection ensures every surface meets our quality standards.

How much does pressure washing cost in Farragut, TN?

Pressure washing costs in Farragut typically range from $150 to $500 depending on the surface type and square footage. Is pressure washing safe for Farragut homes with vinyl siding or brick?

Yes. We use soft wash techniques with eco-friendly detergents for delicate surfaces like vinyl siding, painted wood, and brick commonly found in Farragut neighborhoods. High-pressure cleaning is reserved for concrete driveways and walkways. Our team adjusts pressure and technique for each surface to prevent damage.

How often should Farragut homeowners schedule pressure washing?

We recommend annual house washing and bi-annual driveway cleaning for most Farragut properties.
