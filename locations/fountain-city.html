Fountain City Pressure Washing

Fountain City is one of Knoxville's most established neighborhoods — named for the natural springs that once drew visitors to the area. Today it's known for its charming mix of mid-century homes, tree-canopied streets, the Fountain City Park, and the community pride that keeps this neighborhood vibrant. With homes dating from the 1930s through the 1970s, exterior surfaces need regular cleaning to stay looking their best.

The area's mature oak and maple trees are gorgeous — but they drop leaves, sap, and pollen that stain driveways, clog gutters, and feed algae growth on siding and walkways. Our pressure washing brings Fountain City homes back to life.

Services

  • House washing — Gentle soft wash for vintage and modern homes
  • Driveway & sidewalk cleaning — Remove leaf stains and algae
  • Deck cleaning — Restore screened porches and open decks
  • Fence cleaning — Wood privacy fences and pickets
  • Roof cleaning — Safe shingle and metal roof washing

Serving Fountain City

We cover all of Fountain City including the neighborhoods along Broadway, Hotel Road, Essary Road, and the areas surrounding Fountain City Park and Fountain City Lake. Also serving nearby communities of Inskip, Norwood, and north Knoxville.
