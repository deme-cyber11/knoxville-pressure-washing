Powell's Trusted Pressure Washing Team

Powell is a welcoming northern Knox County community of approximately 15,000 residents, centered around the historic Powell Station area. With Norris Lake nearby, family-oriented neighborhoods, and a healthy mix of established homes and new developments, Powell offers a quality of life that attracts families from across the region. Tennessee's humid summers, heavy pollen seasons, and proximity to wooded hillsides mean exterior surfaces accumulate algae, mildew, dirt, and organic staining faster than most homeowners expect.

Knox Pressure Pros delivers professional, eco-friendly pressure washing throughout the Powell area. Whether your home sits along Emory Road, in one of Powell's newer subdivisions, or on a wooded lot near Norris Lake, we restore and protect exterior surfaces with results that last.

Our Pressure Washing Process

  1. Free Property Assessment — We evaluate your surfaces, identify problem spots, and deliver a transparent quote with no hidden fees.
  2. Equipment Setup & Area Prep — Landscaping is protected, fixtures are covered, and our professional-grade equipment is staged for efficient cleaning.
  3. Pre-Treatment Application — Biodegradable detergents are applied to dissolve algae, mold, mildew, and embedded stains before pressure washing begins.
  4. High-Pressure Cleaning — We calibrate pressure for each surface type, removing years of buildup without causing damage to your property.
  5. Final Rinse & Inspection — A complete rinse and detailed walk-through confirm every surface meets our quality standards before we leave.
