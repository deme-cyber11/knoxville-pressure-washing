Powell Pressure Washing Services

Powell sits in the rolling hills north of Knoxville along Clinton Highway and Emory Road — a mix of established neighborhoods, newer subdivisions, and rural properties. The area's lush vegetation and mature hardwoods are beautiful, but they also mean more organic debris, pollen, and moisture on exterior surfaces throughout the year.

Whether you have a brick ranch on a half-acre lot or a newer home in one of Powell's growing subdivisions, we provide thorough pressure washing that removes years of buildup and restores your property's appearance.

Services for Powell Properties

  • House washing — Soft wash safe for all siding types
  • Driveway cleaning — Concrete and asphalt driveways
  • Deck & patio cleaning — Wood, composite, and stone
  • Fence cleaning — Restore weathered wood and vinyl fences
  • Roof cleaning — Remove black streaks and moss

Why Powell Homes Need Regular Cleaning

The north Knox County hills get slightly more rainfall than downtown Knoxville, and the tree cover keeps surfaces shaded and damp longer. This accelerates algae and mildew growth on driveways, sidewalks, and siding. Homes along Emory Road and the Brickyard communities especially benefit from annual exterior cleaning.

We also see a lot of red clay staining in Powell — the area's distinctive Tennessee clay gets tracked onto driveways and splashed onto foundations during rain. Our cleaning process removes clay staining that regular garden hoses can't touch.
