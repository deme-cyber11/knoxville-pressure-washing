Oak Ridge's Trusted Pressure Washing Experts

Oak Ridge — known as "The Secret City" — was built during World War II as part of the Manhattan Project and remains one of Tennessee's most unique communities. Home to approximately 31,000 residents, Oak Ridge is the site of Oak Ridge National Laboratory, the Y-12 National Security Complex, and the American Museum of Science and Energy. The city's distinctive planned-city layout, with its original mid-century neighborhoods and newer developments, creates a community unlike any other in the Knoxville metro. The surrounding ridges and forested areas, combined with East Tennessee's humid climate, mean exterior surfaces accumulate algae, mold, mildew, and pollen at a steady pace.

Knox Pressure Pros provides licensed, insured, and eco-friendly pressure washing throughout Oak Ridge. From the original "alphabet houses" and cemesto homes built in the 1940s to modern subdivisions, we tailor our cleaning approach to preserve and protect every surface type in this historic city.

Our Pressure Washing Process

  1. Free Property Assessment — We inspect your property, evaluate surface types and conditions, and deliver a transparent, no-obligation quote.
  2. Equipment Setup & Area Prep — We protect landscaping, cover outdoor fixtures, and stage commercial-grade equipment for efficient, safe cleaning.
  3. Pre-Treatment Application — Eco-friendly, biodegradable detergents break down algae, mold, mildew, and embedded stains before washing begins.
  4. High-Pressure Cleaning — Pressure is calibrated for each surface — gentle for historic siding and brick, stronger for concrete driveways and walkways.
  5. Final Rinse & Inspection — A thorough rinse and walk-through inspection ensure every surface meets our quality standards before we pack up.
