Oak Ridge is a city of approximately 31,000 residents with a history unlike any other community in East Tennessee. Known as the "Secret City" for its role in the Manhattan Project during World War II, Oak Ridge was built from the ground up in the 1940s and remains home to major Department of Energy facilities including Oak Ridge National Laboratory. The city features a distinctive mix of original wartime-era homes, mid-century neighborhoods, and modern developments that have expanded along the Oak Ridge Turnpike and Illinois Avenue corridors. Oak Ridge is surrounded by protected greenbelts and forested ridgelines that give the city its name but also create shaded, moisture-rich conditions where algae, mold, and mildew thrive on exterior surfaces.
