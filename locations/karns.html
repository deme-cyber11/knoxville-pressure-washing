Karns Pressure Washing Services

Karns is a growing community in northwest Knox County, with a mix of newer subdivisions, established neighborhoods, and rural properties along Oak Ridge Highway and Byington-Solway Road. The area's family-friendly atmosphere and proximity to both Knoxville and Oak Ridge make it a popular place to settle — and with Tennessee's humidity, those properties need regular exterior maintenance.

We provide pressure washing for Karns homeowners who want to keep their homes looking great without spending their weekends with a garden hose and a brush.

Services

  • House washing — All siding types, soft wash safe
  • Driveway cleaning — Concrete and aggregate
  • Deck & patio cleaning — Wood and composite
  • Fence cleaning — Privacy fences, split rail, vinyl
  • Roof cleaning — Shingle and metal
  • Commercial — Businesses along Oak Ridge Highway

Karns Area Coverage

We serve all of Karns including neighborhoods along Oak Ridge Highway, Byington-Solway Road, Ball Camp Pike, and Beaver Ridge Road. Also covering nearby Hardin Valley and the communities between Karns and Farragut.
