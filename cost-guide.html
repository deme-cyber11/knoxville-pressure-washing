What Affects Pressure Washing Cost?

  • Square footage — Larger areas take more time and resources
  • Surface type — Concrete is straightforward; delicate surfaces like roofs require soft washing
  • Level of buildup — Heavy mold, algae, or staining may require pre-treatment
  • Accessibility — Multi-story homes or hard-to-reach areas may cost more
  • Condition — Surfaces that haven't been cleaned in years need more work

Bundle and Save

Most Knoxville homeowners save 10–15% by bundling services. Common packages include:

  • Curb Appeal Package: House wash + driveway + sidewalks
  • Backyard Refresh: Deck + patio + fence
  • Full Property Clean: House + driveway + deck + walkways

Ask about our bundle pricing when you request your estimate.

Why Professional Beats DIY

A consumer pressure washer rental runs $75–$150/day and you still need cleaning solutions, time, and the knowledge to avoid damaging surfaces. Professional service costs a bit more but delivers better results in less time, with no risk of property damage, and includes cleanup.
