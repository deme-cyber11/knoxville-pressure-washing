Simple Process

How Professional Pressure Washing
Actually Works

The skill is knowing how much pressure each surface can handle — and using the right cleaning agents so the water doesn't have to do all the work.

1

Assessment & Estimate

We walk the property, identify surface types, note any damage, and give you a written quote. For most homes this takes 15 minutes.

2

Pre-Treatment

Algae, mold, and mildew respond to biodegradable detergent before water hits. This does the biological work so pressure stays low.

3

Calibrated Wash by Surface

Concrete gets higher pressure and a fan nozzle. Vinyl siding gets lower pressure, top-to-bottom. Wood decks get soft wash to protect the grain.

4

Final Rinse & Inspect

Final rinse removes all detergent residue. We walk the property with you before leaving. No streaks, no residue.

5

Same-Day Transformation

Full property packages in Knoxville finish in 2–4 hours. Come home to a house that looks completely different.
